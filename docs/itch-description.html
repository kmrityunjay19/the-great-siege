Seven Kingdoms: Ancient Adversaries — the classic real-time strategy game, running right in your browser. No install, no download. The full original engine (economy, diplomacy, spies, 10 playable cultures, the AI) compiled to WebAssembly — not a remake.

Build your kingdom, recruit and train units, manage trade and tax, hire independent heroes, run spies into rival courts, and conquer through war or diplomacy.

Controls

  • Left-click to select / interact, right-click to move and command
  • Arrow keys or push the cursor to a screen edge to scroll the map
  • The fullscreen button (top-right) or your browser's fullscreen for big-screen play; Esc exits fullscreen

Tips

  • Loads in two parts — you can start playing while the encyclopedia, scenarios, and tutorial finish downloading in the background.
  • Saves are stored in your browser and survive a reload.
